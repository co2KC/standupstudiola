Questions.

I've never done standup. Where do I start?

You're exactly who we built this for. Most first-timers start with the next group class, or a single private session to get their feet wet.

Do I need material before my first session?

No. Come with a full notebook, half-written ideas, or absolutely nothing. We'll mine your stories, point of view and life experiences together and turn them into jokes you can try on stage.

In person or online?

Both. Group classes and private coaching happen in person in Los Angeles, and many coaches also work over Zoom. Check with your coach when you sign up.

How do I pick a coach?

A great problem to have. Read the specialties, go with your gut, and don't be afraid to try more than one. Only you know what you're looking for and whether the chemistry works.

Do you offer refunds?

Typically no, mostly because classes are small and your spot is one someone else didn't get. That said, each instructor handles their own classes their own way, and we always want to work with you.

Do the instructors work for Standup Studio LA?

Nope. Every instructor is independent. You pay them directly, and purchases, compliments and complaints all go straight to them. Standup Studio LA makes no money off their services and doesn't vet or vouch for them. We just make it easy to find them.
