What is Standup Studio LA?

Standup Studio LA is where students find instructors, and vice versa. It's a simple concept: Instructors post who they are and what they do out here, and folks wanting to learn comedy can get matched to comedy instructors they want to work with. You can book their services right here from the site, and get started on your comedy journey!

Classes can be taught in-person, on Zoom, in group settings or private. It is up to the instructor and the student. If you want to see a class that is not currently being offered, just let us know. We will do our best to find a comedian to fill that void.

Frequently asked questions.

I've never done standup before. Where should I start?

If you've never been on a mic before, you're exactly the person we built Standup Studio LA for. Most first-timers start with either a single private session to get their feet wet or the next available beginner-friendly group class. Standup Studio LA offers numerous beginner-friendly options and instructors so you can find the perfect fit!

Do I need material prepared before my first session?

No. Standup Studio LA will help you through the entire process! You can come with a full notebook, half-written ideas, or absolutely nothing. We'll mine your stories, point of view, and life experiences together and turn them into jokes you can try on stage.

Are sessions in person or online?

The short answer is...both. Standup Studio LA offers private coaching and select group classes in person in Los Angeles, and also over Zoom for remote students. Make sure you check when you register what the instructor's requirements are. They will all note whether they offer a specific class only in-person or via Zoom as well.

What if I'm not sure which coach to pick?

What a great problem to have! Comedy instruction is all about finding the right fit! We encourage you to not only try our instructors, but also our friends in the area. Only you know what you are looking for and how the chemistry could work.

Do you offer refunds?

Standup Studio LA typically does not offer refunds for a variety of reasons, most notably that our class sizes are limited, and by booking, you are potentially taking the space of another student. Having said that, each instructor handles their own classes in their own way. We always want to work with you and make sure you are absolutely delighted!

Are instructors employees of Standup Studio LA?

Nope. Each instructor on the site is their own independent entity, meaning all purchases, negotiations, compliments and complaints go straight to them. Standup Studio LA makes no money off of their services, and does not vouch for or vet them as people or instructors. We are simply making you aware of instructors who offer classes in different niches in your area or on Zoom.
